#  My Favorite Movie: *Avengers: Endgame*

> *"Whatever it takes."* — Captain America


##  Overview
**Avengers: Endgame** is a **superhero film** produced by *Marvel Studios* and directed by *Anthony and Joe Russo*.  
It serves as the epic conclusion to over a decade of interconnected Marvel movies.

##  Main Characters
- **Iron Man (Tony Stark)** – The genius inventor who sacrifices everything.  
- **Captain America (Steve Rogers)** – The brave soldier who never gives up.  
- **Thor** – The god of thunder, struggling to find purpose.  
- **Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)** – The spy with a heart of gold.  
- **Hulk (Bruce Banner)** – The brilliant scientist and green powerhouse.


##  Why I Love It
- Emotional storytelling and closure for beloved heroes.  
- Stunning action scenes and special effects.  
- The *“Avengers Assemble”* moment gives goosebumps every time!
